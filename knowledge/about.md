full-stack products — and likes shipping complete systems, not just models: he owns the

## What he builds

  Hangin' (honest, backtested PM2.5 forecasting dashboard on free public data).
- **Full-stack products:** CafèSync, CLICKSILOG, Smart Scheduling System, and this very
  portfolio — including zeref-bot, the grounded chatbot answering questions about him.
